About RoadGuard AI

Every year, thousands of lives are lost due to preventable road accidents caused by potholes and poor road conditions. RoadGuard AI was created to address this critical safety issue using cutting-edge artificial intelligence technology.

Our mission is to create safer roads for everyone by providing cities and drivers with the tools they need to identify, report, and fix dangerous road conditions before they cause accidents.

By combining computer vision, GPS tracking, and real-time data processing, we're building a smarter, safer transportation infrastructure for the future.
